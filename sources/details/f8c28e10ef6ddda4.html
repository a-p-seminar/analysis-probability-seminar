On exact self-tensor stability for systems Boolean operators and Hankel operators

发布时间：2023-04-17 作者： 浏览次数：
Speaker: 邱彦奇 教授 DateTime: 2023年4月18日（周二）上午10：00—12：00
Brief Introduction to Speaker:

邱彦奇 武汉大学教授 中国科学院数学与系统科学研究院研究员

 Place: 6号楼M109研究生讨论室
Abstract:In our recent characterization of completely bounded Fourier multipliers between H1 and H2 or H1 and BMOA spaces on torus, we introduce a notion of exact self-tensor stability (abbrev. ESTS) for a family of operators. In this talk, firstly, an efficient way of checking ESTS will be explained for a given family of unit norm Boolean matrices or Boolean operators, secondly, the Hankel operators associated with various monoids will be proved in various different ways to have ESTS. Many natural conjectures will also be given. The talk is based on joint work with Yong HAN and Zipeng WANG.
